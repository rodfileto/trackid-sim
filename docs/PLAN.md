# Plan: synthetic data for TrackID research

Goal: a public, reproducible, ground-truthed multimodal corpus + generator that lets
every paper in the portfolio be executed by anyone without police data. Each phase
ends in something runnable and a gate that must pass before the next starts.

| # | Phase | Deliverable | Gate to pass | Unblocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Scaffold** ✅ | Population + onomastic (+ a decision sim, since replaced by trackid's own pipeline), loaded through `identity.Ingest`/`cases.Ingest`, clustered, scored. Idempotent, seeded, safety-guarded. | End-to-end run; 0 unexplained cluster-id changes across waves; deterministic world hash. | P3 (cluster-id stability), P1 (runnable demo) |
| 1 | **Realistic onomastics** ✅ | IBGE Censo 2022 name/surname frequencies (Brazil, a state or a municipality; one snapshot, not by decade) behind `-lexicon`; corruption rates are the user's parameters, not calibrated (both decided in `docs/ROADMAP.md` B); optional Febrl/GeCo cross-check. | Marginal name distributions match the source; collision rate of full names is reported and defensible. | Any linkage claim |
| 3 | **Face layer** | Faces we generate ourselves from Apache-2.0 models: one base face per human (FLUX.1-schnell), variants of that same face (Qwen-Image-Edit) → enrollment photos + degraded "CCTV" captures; documented degradation pipeline; ISO/IEC 29794-5 (OFIQ) quality per image; real embeddings via trackid-vision (YuNet + AuraFace) replace the parametric face scores. | A spike shows identity is preserved (genuine and impostor scores separate); generator licences recorded in `docs/LICENSES.md`; performance reported as a function of measured quality. | P5 (with bias explicitly scoped out); faces in the P0 corpus |
| 4 | **Fingerprint layer** | SFinGe/Anguli-class rolled prints for the KNOWN side; synthetic latents (partial, noisy, distorted) for the QUESTIONED side; minutiae via a documented extractor; SourceAFIS (Java sidecar now, a Go port later) replaces parametric fingerprint scores, as trackid-vision does for faces. | Latent realism stated as a limitation, not claimed. Identity survives the real matcher; genuine/impostor rates reported against SourceAFIS's own reference (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). | Fingerprint side of P2/P4 |
| 5 | **Review exercise** | Students make the human decisions (VERIFICATOR, REVIEWER) in trackid's UI on top of trackid's own SYSTEM decisions, which are all kept; they work trackid's analyses (examination, blind verification, inconsistence), distributed among them by trackid, and take leads to laudos (a comparison decided on the conclusion scale and written as a report); their decisions, conclusions and the cluster revisions they cause scored against the truth (`eval`, `snapshot`). | A class reviews one loaded world and each student's decisions are scored, with uncertainty over students. | P2 (ACE-V), P3 (adversarial revisions) |
| 6 | **Release** | Zenodo DOI, licence (code Apache-2.0, chosen 2026-10-20; corpus planned CC-BY-4.0), versioned corpus + `world.json` + generator + config, pre-registered analysis plan for the eventual real-data replication. | A stranger reproduces the headline tables from the DOI alone. | P0, P1 |

Phase 2 (simulator validation: aggregates against published statistics, and a plausibility panel
of practitioners) was **removed on 2026-10-20**. The world does not need to emulate reality
precisely: the occurrence side follows ISP-RJ's counts by construction, and the offender-side
rates are the user's parameters, like the corruption rates (ROADMAP B). Papers state this as a
limitation. The other phases keep their numbers.

## Principles

- **Truth stays out of the system under test.** Only `world.json` holds it.
- **Always sweep** what the simulator sets (seeds, world sizes, thresholds): no single
  parameter set is reported as "the" result. Human decisions are not simulated, so they
  are not swept; they are reported with uncertainty over students (Phase 5).
- **Say what synthetic cannot show**: absolute field error rates, and demographic
  bias (generators carry their own skew). Keep that line in every manuscript.
- **Two licence tracks.** Research use (papers, this repo) may rely on
  non-commercial terms; the open-source trackid app, which real organizations may
  deploy, may not. So nothing restricted shapes trackid (code, defaults,
  thresholds, fixtures, bundled models) or the released corpus. Operational
  thresholds are each laboratory's own validation, never calibrated here.
- **Synthetic faces, generated by us.** No dataset of real people (FairFace, LFW,
  UTKFace, FFHQ...): they would appear as offenders in a published corpus (LGPD
  sensitive data), and their licences are weaker than they look. No DigiFace1M
  either: it is synthetic but under a non-commercial agreement whose "Results"
  clause could reach whatever is built from it. Generate with models whose licence
  we have read: FLUX.1-schnell and Qwen-Image-Edit (Apache-2.0). Avoid
  FLUX.1-dev / Kontext-dev (non-commercial), InfiniteYou (CC-BY-NC), and identity
  adapters built on InsightFace (non-commercial). Say only that no real individual
  is represented, not that no real data was involved: the generators were trained
  on scraped photos.
- **Legal/licence hygiene**: verify redistribution terms of every image generator
  before releasing the corpus, and record model versions and seeds.
